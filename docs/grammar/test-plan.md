## Key Interactions to Verify

- Fresh guest: one click from /grammar into lesson 1; finish; stars; lesson 2 unlocked after returning
- Wrong answer: explanation shown, card comes back once at the end
- Typed fill on phone: no autocorrect/capitalization
- X mid-lesson asks to confirm; closing tab warns
- Save failure (offline at the end): retry, then "Thử lại" button, result not lost
- TTS button speaks examples; absent when unsupported
- Both themes (light manga and lamp), 375px and desktop

## Edge Cases

- Test-out on a later unit marks earlier units passed with 0 stars, no review fading for them
- Replaying a 2-star lesson for 3 stars updates stars only
- Due review node appears faded next day; stage 7 never fades
- Weak-spot button hidden when nothing is missed
- Unknown card ids after a content edit: "Bài vừa được cập nhật"

## Critical Paths

- Guest lesson 1 -> stars -> unlock (Playwright e2e)
- Sign in after guest progress: progress merged, nothing lost
