## Vision

### 10x Check

Grammar stops being a curriculum you visit and becomes the place your real
mistakes get fixed: Dictation and Writing errors route you to the exact lesson,
the path re-orders itself around what you actually get wrong, and every example
can be heard. v3 lays the rails for that (permanent lesson/card ids, miss log).

## Scope Decisions

| #   | Proposal                                                                                                   | Effort | Decision | Reasoning                                                                  |
| --- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------ | -------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | TTS button on every English example and on the correct answer in feedback (speechSynthesis, same as Vocab) | S      | ACCEPTED | Grammar with intonation, near-zero cost                                    |

| 4   | Dictation -> Grammar bridge (suggest lesson from dictation errors)                                         | L      | DEFERRED | Do after v3 is stable; error-type inference from text diffs is error-prone |
